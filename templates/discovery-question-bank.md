# Discovery Question Bank

Reusable questions for discovery sessions. Pick, adapt, and add your own. Prefer **specific, recent examples** over opinions, and **watch** the work whenever you can.

## Opening and context
- What does a typical day or week look like for you?
- Walk me through the last time you did <task>, step by step.
- Who else is involved before and after you?

## Pain and cost (aim for numbers)
- Which part of this takes the most time? How long, how often?
- What happens when it goes wrong? How often does that happen?
- What does a mistake or delay cost: money, customers, rework?
- How do you know it's going well or badly? What do you measure?

## History
- Have you tried to fix this before? What happened?
- What tools do you use today, and what do you like or hate about them?

## Data
- Where does the information for this task come from?
- Who owns that data? Who can grant access?
- How accurate and complete is it? Can you show me an example?
- Is any of it personal, confidential or regulated?

## Constraints
- What budget, timeline or staffing limits do we need to respect?
- Which IT, security or legal rules apply? Who reviews new systems?
- Who will maintain this after the project ends?

## Success
- If this worked perfectly, what would be different in six months?
- Which number would you show your boss to prove it worked?
- What would make you stop using a new tool after a week?

## Testing the requested solution
- When you picture <the requested solution>, what problem does it solve for you?
- If we couldn't build that, what would you want instead?

## Closing
- What should I have asked but didn't?
- Who else should I talk to?
- Can I come back and watch you do this for an hour?

## Things to observe (not ask)
- Workarounds: sticky notes, personal spreadsheets, copy-paste between systems
- Waiting time between steps
- Interruptions
- The difference between what people *say* they do and what they *actually* do

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- ❌ Leading questions: "Wouldn't it be great if an AI did this?"
- ❌ Hypotheticals: "Would you use…?" (People say yes to be polite.)
- ❌ Pitching solutions during discovery
- ❌ Talking more than 20% of the time
