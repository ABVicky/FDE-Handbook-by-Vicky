Module 12 — Field Track: Mock interviews

Time: ~4 hours with partners · Capabilities: C1–C5

  1. Coding (45 min): two problems from coding.md, out loud, with a partner interrupting with questions.
  2. System design (45 min): a prompt from system-design.md you haven't practised; the partner plays a client who answers clarifying questions only if asked.
  3. Client scenario (30 min): one script from client-scenarios.md; a third person observes with the rubric.

After each: one thing to keep, one to change, and a second attempt a week later.

Done when

  • [ ] All three completed with written feedback
  • [ ] Repeated the weakest one and improved its rubric score
