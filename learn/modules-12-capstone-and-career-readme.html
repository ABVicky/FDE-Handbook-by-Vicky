Module 12 — Capstone, Portfolio and Career

💡 In plain words: This is where you show you can do it on your own. You'll pick a new client problem and take it all the way, from the first conversation to a working, handed-over system. Then you'll prepare for real FDE interviews.

🛠️ What you'll build: Your own capstone project, a portfolio that shows your work, and practice for every kind of FDE interview.

⏭️ You can skip for now: Nothing. This is the finish line.

🧭 Comes after: Module 11: Business and Client Engineering · Leads to: your first FDE role 🎉

🪜 How to work through it: read the lessons in order → try the exercises → do the build → do the break & fix → do the Field Track → tick the "Done when" list. New here? Read Start Here first.

Stage: 4 — Field-ready · Time: ~4–6 weeks · Builds: C1–C5 Prerequisites: Modules 01–11.

Outcome

You have independent evidence that you can do the work of an FDE (a capstone taken from a vague problem to a deployed, evaluated, used and handed-over system), a portfolio that shows it, and the preparation to demonstrate it in interviews.

The capstone

capstones/: four briefs (knowledge assistant with permissions, invoice automation with human review, analytics copilot with safe SQL, or your own client), the required deliverables, and the grading rubric.

Interview preparation

interview-prep/: FDE-flavoured coding problems with runnable solutions, system design (with a worked answer), debugging practice, client-scenario role-plays, take-home strategy and behavioural questions.

Field Track

See field-track.md: three mock interviews, including a client-scenario role-play.

Done when (field-ready)

  • [ ] Capstone deployed, evaluated, used by at least one real or realistic user, and handed over, with all eleven deliverables
  • [ ] Portfolio: flagship + two supporting projects with the seven-section README
  • [ ] Three mock interviews completed, including a client scenario, with feedback acted on
  • [ ] Résumé and LinkedIn updated with impact-based bullets
  • [ ] All five capabilities at level 3 or higher, and two at level 4, with evidence links in progress/self-assessment.md
