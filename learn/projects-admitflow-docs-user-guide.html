AdmitFlow — Guide for counsellors

Version: Module 11 · Questions: Priya (AdmitFlow champion), or Mr. Fernandes

Note for learners: this guide describes the finished workflow. In the reference dashboard (web/), the screens for Google sign-in, drafting/approving messages, adding enquiries and the team view are build stretch goals; the API endpoints behind them all exist and are tested. Update the guide to match what your dashboard actually has before handing it to users.

Sign in

Open AdmitFlow in Chrome or Edge and choose Sign in with Google using your Northbridge account. (If Google sign-in isn't set up yet, use the username and password Mr. Okafor gave you.) You stay signed in for the working day.

Start your day: what's due

  1. At the top you'll see how many follow-ups are due in the next 24 hours.
  2. Tick Only mine to see the leads assigned to you, newest first.
  3. Leads waiting more than a day are the most urgent: students contacted within a day enrol about three times as often.

Find a student

Type part of a name, phone number, email or course in the search box. Names with accents (Lucía, Nguyễn) work as typed or without accents in the email.

After every call: add a note and update the status

  1. Click the student's row to open their details.
  2. Type what happened in What happened on this call? and click Add note.
  3. Change Status (new → contacted → visit booked → applied → enrolled, or lost).

Tip: one short note per call is enough: "Interested in evening batch; visiting Saturday."

Schedule a follow-up

In the student's details, choose a date and time and click Schedule follow-up. It appears in your "due" count when the time comes.

Send a follow-up message (with the assistant)

  1. In the student's details, ask the assistant to draft a follow-up.
  2. Read the draft carefully. It uses only our course information, but you know the student.
  3. Approve it if it's right, or reject it and write your own. Nothing is sent until you approve it.
  4. Approved messages are sent within a few minutes by email or WhatsApp.

Reception: add a phone or walk-in enquiry

Use Add enquiry, choose the channel (phone or walk-in), and enter the name and a phone number or email. If the student is already in AdmitFlow, the enquiry is added to their record instead of creating a duplicate.

FAQ

  • "The fee the assistant gave looks wrong." Tell Mr. Fernandes straight away and don't send it. The assistant only uses the fee schedule in AdmitFlow, so the document may need updating.
  • "I can't change a lead." It's assigned to another counsellor. Ask Mr. Fernandes to reassign it.
  • "Do I still need my spreadsheet / the Friday report?" No. Mr. Fernandes uses the team view in AdmitFlow.
