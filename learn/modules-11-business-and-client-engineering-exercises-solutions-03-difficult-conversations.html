Exercise 03 — Model answers

1. Late (to Ms. Rao):

I want to flag a risk early. WhatsApp's business messaging approval for our message templates won't arrive before the intake opens. I applied three weeks ago, and approval times are outside our control. Follow-ups will go by email from day one, and WhatsApp will switch on the day approval arrives, with no other work needed. For the board, I'd suggest: "Follow-ups launch by email on [date]; WhatsApp follows once the provider approves our templates." I'll update you as soon as I hear.

2. "Just add it" (to Mr. Fernandes):

That's a nice idea, and students would appreciate it. Two things make me want to wait: we don't collect birthdays today, so we'd need a new form field and the students' consent, and adding it now would push go-live back by about a week, just before the intake. Could we add it to the list for next term, with the consent question built into the new enquiry form? I'll write it up so we don't lose it.

3. Your mistake (to counsellors and Mr. Fernandes):

This morning a database update I ran wiped 40 call notes. That was my mistake: I hadn't tested the update against a copy of the real data. Within two hours I restored every note from last night's backup; any note added between midnight and 9:40 this morning may need re-entering. Here's the list of affected leads. From now on, every update is tested on a copy of live data first, and I'll send the full incident report by Friday. I'm sorry for the extra work.

What these have in common: said early, plainly, impact first, own your part, a plan with dates, a written follow-up.
