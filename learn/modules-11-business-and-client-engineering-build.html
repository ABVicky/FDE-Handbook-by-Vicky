Module 11 Build — AdmitFlow engagement close-out

Time: ~15–20 hours · Capabilities: C1, C5

Scenario

AdmitFlow has run for a full intake. Ms. Rao has a board meeting in two weeks and wants to present "what we got for the money" and whether to fund phase 2. Then the engagement moves to support.

Deliverables

  1. Business case and results (2–3 pages): baseline vs measured outcomes; the ROI model with assumptions labelled measured/client/guess; three scenarios; what phase 2 would add. Start from roi_model.py.
  2. Board presentation (≤ 8 slides, 10 minutes): the problem, what changed, the numbers, what we learned (including what didn't work, e.g. lead scoring), the phase 2 proposal with its cost and expected value. Present to peers playing the board; take questions.
  3. Training material: user guide per role (template), a one-page quick reference, and two short screen recordings.
  4. Handover document (template) with owners, access transfer, support model, known issues and next steps.
  5. Lessons learned: a one-page retrospective with the client.

Done when

  • [ ] Every number in the business case traces to data or a labelled assumption
  • [ ] The presentation leads with the conservative case and states limitations
  • [ ] Someone who's never used AdmitFlow completes three tasks using only the user guide
  • [ ] Someone else answers "the outbox alert fired, what now?" using only the handover pack
  • [ ] Lessons learned include at least two things you'd do differently
