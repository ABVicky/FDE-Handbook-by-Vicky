Admissions process

Eligibility

Applicants need to have completed secondary school (Class 12 or equivalent). Nursing Assistance applicants must be at least 18 years old.

How to apply

Apply online on the institute's website or at either campus. You will need a photo ID, your school certificates and a passport-size photograph.

Intakes

There are two intakes a year: September and February. Applications for the September intake close on 15 August.

Campus visits

Prospective students can book a campus visit with a counsellor. Visits take about 45 minutes and include a tour and a meeting with a lecturer.
