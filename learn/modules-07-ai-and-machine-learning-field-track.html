Module 07 — Field Track: Tell the director the truth about the model

Time: ~1.5 hours · Capabilities: C1, C5

Why

Ms. Rao is excited about AI and has told the board it's coming. Your analysis shows the model adds little, and that response speed matters far more. Delivering an unwelcome finding well (clearly, with evidence, with a constructive alternative) is one of the most valuable things an FDE does.

The task

Write a one-page memo to Ms. Rao (my-work/field-track/m07-model-memo.md):

  1. Headline in one sentence
  2. What we tested, in plain words (no "AUC")
  3. What we found: the model's modest gain, in counsellor terms ("about 15% more future students reached on the first day than random order")
  4. The bigger finding: response time (16% vs 2.5%), with the honest caveat that it's correlation
  5. Recommendation: e.g. a pilot target of first contact within 24 hours, extra cover for out-of-hours enquiries, the score as a tie-breaker
  6. How we'll know it worked: a metric and a date
  7. What to tell the board: two sentences she can reuse

Then test it: give it to a non-technical person, ask them to explain the recommendation back, and revise.

Done when

  • [ ] No unexplained technical terms
  • [ ] Every number has a comparison
  • [ ] One clear recommendation with a measurable target
  • [ ] Revised after a real reader's feedback
