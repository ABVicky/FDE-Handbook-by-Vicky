Module 01 — Checkpoint

Answer all 10 questions before opening the answers. Pass mark: 8 / 10, and you must also complete the build task to skip the module.

1. Which best describes a Forward Deployed Engineer?

  • a) A salesperson with technical knowledge who runs product demos
  • b) A software engineer who works directly with customers to build and deploy solutions in their environment
  • c) A consultant who recommends which software a company should buy
  • d) A developer who works remotely from a customer's office

2. An institute's director asks for "an AI chatbot." What should an FDE do first?

  • a) Pick an LLM provider and start building
  • b) Politely decline because chatbots are overused
  • c) Find out what problem the chatbot is meant to solve and for whom
  • d) Send a price estimate

3. Which is the best success metric for an FDE engagement?

  • a) Number of features delivered
  • b) Lines of code written
  • c) Percentage of enquiries contacted within 24 hours, before vs. after
  • d) Customer said the demo looked great

4. Name the eight lifecycle stages in order. (Short answer)

5. What is the main difference between a solutions engineer and an FDE?

  • a) Solutions engineers write more code
  • b) Solutions engineers mainly help win the sale; FDEs are accountable for what runs in production after it
  • c) FDEs never talk to customers before the contract is signed
  • d) There is no difference

6. Why do many AI prototypes fail to reach production? Give three reasons. (Short answer)

7. A job posting says: "lead discovery workshops, advise on AI strategy, partner with account executives, 50% travel." Where does it sit on the engineering ↔ consulting spectrum?

  • a) Strongly engineering-heavy
  • b) Balanced
  • c) Leaning consulting-heavy
  • d) Impossible to tell

8. Which of these is a common failure at the Discover stage?

  • a) Spending time watching users work
  • b) Solving the problem the sponsor described rather than the one users have
  • c) Asking for sample data
  • d) Mapping stakeholders

9. True or false: an FDE should hide the prototype's failure cases during a demo to keep the customer confident.

10. Give one way an FDE's work benefits their own company's product, beyond the single customer. (Short answer)

Answers (open only after answering)
  1. b
  2. c: understand the problem before choosing a solution.
  3. c: measurable, tied to the business outcome, with a baseline.
  4. Discover → Define → Design → Prototype → Validate → Deploy → Monitor → Scale & hand over.
  5. b
  6. Any three of: messy real data; accuracy not measured; permissions and security; integration with existing tools; no monitoring; users don't trust or adopt it; unclear success criteria.
  7. c: workshops, advising, sales partnership and heavy travel suggest consulting-heavy.
  8. b
  9. False. Showing limitations builds trust and leads to realistic acceptance criteria.
  10. Examples: patterns built for one customer become product features; FDEs report real customer needs to the product team; reusable templates speed up future deployments.
