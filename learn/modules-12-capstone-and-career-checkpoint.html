Module 12 — Readiness check

There's no quiz for this module. Instead, answer these honestly, with links to evidence. If you can't link evidence, that's your next piece of work.

#Can you show…Evidence link
1a problem statement you wrote from real discovery, that names no solution?
2a deployed system someone else used, and what happened when they did?
3an evaluation with numbers against a baseline, including where it failed?
4a security review and the tests behind its controls?
5an alert that would catch a real failure, and the runbook entry for it?
6a business case with labelled assumptions and a conservative scenario?
7a handover someone else used without asking you?
8a difficult message you wrote to a client: a delay, a failure, or a "no"?
9three mock interviews and what you changed after them?
10your self-assessment at level 3+ on all five capabilities?

Eight or more with evidence: start applying. Fewer: pick the gaps and close them, one by one.
