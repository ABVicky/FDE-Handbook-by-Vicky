Use job descriptions (Module 01) to check demand where you want to work.

Keep learning on the job

  • Keep a work journal: problems met, how you solved them, what you'd do differently. It becomes interview stories and blog posts.
  • After each engagement, write a reusable asset (template, connector, checklist), which is the FDE habit of feeding the product.
  • Re-read job descriptions every six months; close the gaps that keep appearing.

Open source

Contributing to the tools you use (documentation fixes, bug reports with reproductions, small features) builds skill, reputation and a network. This handbook welcomes contributions too (CONTRIBUTING.md): sourced case studies, job-description snapshots, new exercises.

Where FDE careers go

Senior and lead FDE; FDE manager; solutions or enterprise architect; product engineering or product management; founding a company, often from problems seen across many clients (Module 01, Lesson 07). Whichever way you go, the combination you've practised (engineering depth with business judgement) stays valuable.

Check yourself

Write your 12-month plan: target role and track, two job descriptions you're aiming for, the three biggest gaps, and how you'll close each (project, course, contribution).
