Module 11 — Business and Client Engineering

💡 In plain words: This module is about people and business, and it's what separates an FDE from an engineer who happens to sit near a client. You'll learn to show the value of your work in money and time, communicate with different people, and make sure your system is actually used.

🛠️ What you'll build: The documents that close a project: the business case, a presentation for the director's board, a user guide, and a proper handover.

⏭️ You can skip for now: Nothing essential. If you already work in consulting, take the checkpoint.

🧭 Comes after: Module 10: Production Engineering, Security and Reliability · Leads to: Module 12: Capstone, Portfolio and Career

🪜 How to work through it: read the lessons in order → try the exercises → do the build → do the break & fix → do the Field Track → tick the "Done when" list. New here? Read Start Here first.

Stage: 4 — Field-ready · Time: ~2 weeks (plus the Field Track throughout) · Builds: C1 (Understand the business), C5 (Lead the engagement) Prerequisites: Modules 01–10 (this module consolidates the Field Track exercises from every earlier module).

Outcome

After this module you can run an engagement end to end, from discovery to handover: understand how the client's business creates value, map and fix processes, keep scope under control, build an honest business case, communicate with every stakeholder in their terms, drive adoption, and hand over a system the client can own.

Why this matters to an FDE

The break & fix in this module is a project where every requirement was delivered and tested, and it still failed: only two of six counsellors used it. That's the most common way FDE engagements fail. Not bad code, but a process nobody switched off, training nobody could attend, and "done" declared on go-live day. The skills here are what separate an FDE from an engineer who happens to be on site.

Checkpoint

Experienced in consulting or delivery? Take the checkpoint. Score ≥ 80% and complete the build and break & fix to move on.
