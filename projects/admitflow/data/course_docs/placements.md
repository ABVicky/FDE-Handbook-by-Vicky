# Placements and careers

## Placement assistance

The institute offers placement assistance: interview preparation, CV workshops and introductions to partner employers. We do not guarantee a job.

## Outcomes

Of the students who completed a diploma in 2025, 78% found a job related to their course within six months.

## Internships

Web Development and Data Analytics students complete a four-week internship with a partner company in their final term.
