# Courses and timings

## Data Analytics

A one-year diploma covering spreadsheets, SQL, Python and data visualization. Classes run on weekdays, 10am to 1pm, at the North campus. No prior programming experience is needed, but applicants take a short numeracy test.

## Web Development

A one-year diploma covering HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React. Weekday classes, 2pm to 5pm, at the North campus. Students need their own laptop.

## Digital Marketing

A six-month certificate covering social media, search advertising and analytics. Weekday batch 10am to 1pm, and an evening batch 6pm to 9pm, at the South campus.

## Accounting

A one-year diploma covering bookkeeping, taxation and accounting software. Weekday batch 10am to 1pm, and an evening batch 6pm to 9pm, at the South campus.

## Business Administration

A one-year diploma covering management, marketing, finance and communication. Weekday classes, 2pm to 5pm, at the South campus.

## Nursing Assistance

A one-year diploma with classroom teaching and supervised hospital placements. Weekday classes, 9am to 3pm, at the North campus. Applicants must be at least 18 and pass a health check before placements.
