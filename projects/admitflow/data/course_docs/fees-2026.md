# Fee schedule 2026–27

All fees are per term. The academic year has three terms. Fees apply to students starting from the 2026 intake.

## Diploma fees

| Course | Fee per term |
| --- | --- |
| Data Analytics | 1,800 |
| Web Development | 1,650 |
| Digital Marketing | 1,400 |
| Accounting | 1,250 |
| Business Administration | 1,500 |
| Nursing Assistance | 1,350 |

## Instalments

Fees can be paid in three instalments per term with no extra charge. The first instalment is due before the term starts.

## Scholarships

Merit scholarships of 10% or 25% of the tuition fee are available. Applications close two weeks before each intake. Scholarships are decided by the admissions committee, not by counsellors.

## Refunds

Fees are refunded in full if a student withdraws before the first day of term, and 50% if they withdraw within the first two weeks. No refunds are given after two weeks.
