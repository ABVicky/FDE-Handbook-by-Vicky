# Frequently asked questions

## Are there evening or weekend classes?

Yes. Accounting and Digital Marketing have evening batches from 6pm to 9pm on weekdays at the South campus. There are no weekend classes.

## Will I get a job after the course?

We offer placement assistance but do not guarantee a job. Of the students who completed a diploma in 2025, 78% found a job related to their course within six months.

## What is the minimum age?

Most courses have no minimum age beyond completing secondary school. Nursing Assistance students must be at least 18 years old.

## Do I need my own laptop?

Web Development students need their own laptop. For other courses, computers are available in the campus labs.

## Can I switch courses after joining?

You can switch to another course within the first two weeks of term if there are seats available. Speak to your counsellor.

## Is there hostel or accommodation?

The institute does not provide accommodation. The admissions office can share a list of nearby hostels.
