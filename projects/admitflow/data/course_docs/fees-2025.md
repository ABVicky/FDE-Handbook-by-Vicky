# Fee schedule 2025–26

All fees are per term. The academic year has three terms.

## Diploma fees

| Course | Fee per term |
| --- | --- |
| Data Analytics | 1,650 |
| Web Development | 1,550 |
| Digital Marketing | 1,300 |
| Accounting | 1,150 |
| Business Administration | 1,400 |
| Nursing Assistance | 1,250 |

## Instalments

Fees can be paid in two instalments per term.
