# Capstone D — Bring your own client

Find a real organization with a real problem: a small business, an NGO, a school or college department, a clinic, a club. Your Module 11 Field Track is a good start.

## Rules
- A real person agrees to act as the client, give you an hour a week, and try what you build
- You agree **in writing** what data you may use; never take real personal data without permission and a safe way to handle it (synthetic or anonymized data is usually better)
- The problem must need at least **two** of: data integration, a web application, an AI component, an integration with an existing system
- Scope it to what you can finish in 4–6 weeks; write the out-of-scope list early

## Proposal (approve with a mentor or peer before building)
One page: client, problem, who has it, baseline, success metric, proposed solution, data, risks, what you will **not** build.

The full deliverables and rubric are in the [capstone README](README.md).
