# Lesson 03 — FDE interviews

**Time:** ~6 hours (plus practice) · **Core**

## The usual loop

| Stage | What it tests | Prepare with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter screen | Motivation, communication, logistics (travel, location) | Your 2-minute story: why FDE, what you've built |
| Coding | Fundamentals, clean code, talking while coding | [Coding problems](../../../interview-prep/coding.md) |
| System design | Realistic architecture for a client | [System design guide](../../../interview-prep/system-design.md) |
| Debugging / take-home | Working in unfamiliar code; end-to-end delivery | [Debugging](../../../interview-prep/debugging.md), [take-home](../../../interview-prep/take-home.md) |
| Client scenario | Discovery, judgement, calm under pressure | [Client scenarios](../../../interview-prep/client-scenarios.md) |
| Behavioural | Ownership, conflict, learning | [Behavioural](../../../interview-prep/behavioural.md) |

Companies weight these differently. Engineering-heavy FDE roles emphasize coding and system design; consulting-heavy ones emphasize the case and scenario rounds (Module 01, Lesson 06).

## A four-week preparation plan

| Week | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Two coding problems a day (out loud, timed); polish your portfolio READMEs |
| 2 | Two system-design prompts with a partner; one client-scenario role-play |
| 3 | A take-home under time pressure; six behavioural stories written and rehearsed |
| 4 | Three full mock interviews (one of each kind); research each target company's product and customers |

## During the interview

- **Think out loud.** Silence reads as stuck.
- **Ask clarifying questions** before solving. For FDE roles, this *is* part of the test.
- **State trade-offs** and pick one.
- **Admit what you don't know**, then reason about it.
- **Bring your evidence:** "In my capstone, the evaluation showed…" beats generic answers.

## Questions to ask them

- What does a typical engagement look like, from start to handover?
- How much is engineering vs customer work, really? How much travel?
- How do FDE learnings get back into the product?
- How is success measured for FDEs here?

## Check yourself

Complete three mock interviews: one coding, one system design, one client scenario. Ask each interviewer for one thing to keep and one to change.
