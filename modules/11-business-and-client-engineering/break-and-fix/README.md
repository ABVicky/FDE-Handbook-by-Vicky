# Module 11 — Break & Fix: Delivered to spec, and failing

**Time:** 2 hours · Do this after [Lesson 06](../lessons/06-delivery-and-change.md).

## The situation

AdmitFlow went live for all six counsellors eight weeks ago. Every requirement in the specification was delivered and tested. The FDE declared the engagement complete on go-live day.

Now Ms. Rao writes: *"Only 52% of enquiries were contacted within 24 hours. That's barely better than before. The board asked me what we got for the money."*

Nothing is technically broken. Your evidence:
- [`evidence/usage_weekly.csv`](evidence/usage_weekly.csv): notes added and leads updated per counsellor per week
- [`evidence/interviews.md`](evidence/interviews.md): 15-minute conversations with each counsellor, reception and Mr. Fernandes
- [`evidence/emails.md`](evidence/emails.md): three emails

## Your job

1. **Analyse the usage data:** who adopted, who is drifting back, who never started?
2. **Find the root causes.** There are at least six, and only one or two are about software. Classify each: *process, training, product, communication, ownership*.
3. **Write a recovery plan** for the next four weeks: actions, owners (including the client's people), and how you'll measure success.
4. **Reply to Ms. Rao** (≤ 200 words): honest, no blame on counsellors or on Mr. Fernandes, with the plan and a date to check results.
5. **Write down what you'd do differently** from the start of the engagement.
