# Module 11 — Field Track: A real engagement, in miniature

**Time:** ~8–10 hours over two weeks · **Capabilities:** C1, C5

The capstone of every Field Track exercise so far. Do it with a **real** person and a **real** workflow: a small business, a club, a clinic, a college department, a friend's workplace.

## Steps

1. **Discovery** (Modules 01, 05): two interviews plus 1 hour observing the work. Ask for any data or documents they already use.
2. **Process map** (Lesson 02): current state with pain points quantified; root cause.
3. **Proposal** (Lessons 03–04): the problem statement; a solution sized to what's realistic (it may be a spreadsheet, a form, an automation, or a small app); requirements with acceptance criteria; a cost and value estimate with assumptions; what's out of scope.
4. **Present it** (Lesson 05): 15 minutes with the person, answering their questions. Ask them directly: "Would you use this? What would stop you?"
5. **Revise** and send the final one-page proposal.
6. **Reflect:** what surprised you, what you'd ask earlier next time, and where you were tempted to propose technology before understanding the problem.

## Done when

- [ ] Real person, real workflow, observation included
- [ ] Proposal with measurable success criteria and honest assumptions
- [ ] Presented and revised from their feedback
- [ ] Reflection written

If they want it built, that's a strong candidate for your [Module 12 capstone](../12-capstone-and-career/README.md).
