# Exercise 03 — Three difficult conversations

Write what you'd say (≤ 150 words each), then role-play one with a partner who pushes back:

1. **Late:** the WhatsApp templates won't be approved before the intake opens. The director announced WhatsApp follow-ups to the board.
2. **"Just add it":** Mr. Fernandes asks, two weeks before go-live, for automatic birthday messages to every enquirer.
3. **Your mistake:** a migration you ran overwrote 40 counsellor notes with blanks. You restored them from the backup within 2 hours, but counsellors noticed.
