# Exercise 01 — Map the campus-visit process

Northbridge offers campus visits (Module 04, Exercise 01). Today they're booked by phone, written in a paper diary at each campus, and students often don't turn up.

1. Interview (or role-play) a counsellor and a receptionist about how visits work today.
2. Draw the **current state** as a swimlane diagram (student, counsellor, reception, lecturer). Mark delays, hand-offs, duplication and pain points.
3. Do a **5 Whys** on "students don't turn up."
4. Draw the **future state**, and a **starts / stops** table per role.
5. Estimate the cost of the main pain point (frequency × time, or lost enrolments).
