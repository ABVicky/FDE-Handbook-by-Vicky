# Module 01 — Field Track: Your first real discovery interview

**Time:** ~3 hours (1 h interview, 2 h write-up) · **Capabilities:** C1, C5

AdmitFlow is fictional. This exercise isn't. You'll talk to a real person about a real problem, because interviewing is a skill you only learn by doing it.

## 1. Find someone

Anyone who does repetitive work: a shop owner, a teacher, a receptionist, a clinic administrator, a relative who runs a small business, a friend in an office job. Ask for 30–45 minutes.

**Be honest about why:** "I'm learning how to understand business problems as part of a technical course. Could I ask you about how you do part of your work? I won't build or sell anything, and I won't share your name."

## 2. Prepare (30 min)

Pick one area of their work to focus on. Write 8–10 open questions. Some that work almost everywhere:

- "Walk me through what you did yesterday from when you arrived."
- "Which part of your week feels like the biggest waste of time?"
- "Can you show me how you do that?" *(Watching beats asking.)*
- "How often does that happen? How long does it take each time?"
- "What happens when it goes wrong?"
- "Have you tried to fix it before? What happened?"
- "If you had a magic wand, what would change?" *(Listen to the problem, not the proposed solution.)*

## 3. Interview (30–45 min)

- Listen more than you talk (aim for 80/20).
- Ask for **specific recent examples**, not general opinions.
- Ask for **numbers**: how often, how long, how many.
- Don't propose solutions. Not even "Have you tried an app for that?"
- Take notes on exact phrases. They're gold later.
- End with: "Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?"

## 4. Write up (1–2 h)

Create `my-work/field-track/m01-interview.md` with:

1. **Context:** role and type of organization (no names unless you have permission)
2. **Current workflow:** the steps they described, as a numbered list or simple diagram
3. **Pain points:** each with frequency and impact
4. **Quotes:** 3–5 exact phrases
5. **Problem statement:** using [`templates/problem-statement.md`](../../templates/problem-statement.md)
6. **What you'd ask next:** five follow-up questions
7. **Reflection:** what went well, what you'd do differently, where you were tempted to jump to a solution

## Done when

- [ ] You interviewed a real person for at least 30 minutes
- [ ] Your write-up has a workflow, pain points with numbers, and a solution-free problem statement
- [ ] Your reflection names at least one thing you'll do differently next time

## Privacy and respect

Don't record without explicit permission. Don't write down personal data you don't need. If you share your write-up publicly, anonymize it fully.
