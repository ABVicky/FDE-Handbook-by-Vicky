# Module 01 Build — AdmitFlow Discovery Pack

**Time:** 4–6 hours · **Capabilities:** C1, C5

## Scenario

Read the [AdmitFlow client brief](../../projects/admitflow/BRIEF.md). The director wants "an AI chatbot." Before anyone builds anything, your job is to understand the real problem and define what success looks like.

## What to produce

Create a folder `my-work/admitflow/m01/` (in your own fork) containing four documents. Use the templates linked below.

### 1. Stakeholder map — `stakeholders.md`
For each person in the brief (and anyone missing you think should be involved):

| Stakeholder | Role | What they care about | Influence (H/M/L) | Affected by change (H/M/L) | What could make them resist? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Then answer: **who is missing from the brief?** (Hint: think about who actually *uses* the enquiry process.)

Template: [`templates/stakeholder-map.md`](../../templates/stakeholder-map.md)

### 2. Discovery questions — `discovery-questions.md`
Write **15 questions** you'd ask in a second round of meetings, grouped by stakeholder. Include at least:
- 3 questions that would produce a **number** (a baseline)
- 2 questions about **data**: where it is, who owns it, how clean it is
- 2 questions about **constraints**: budget, IT, legal, timeline
- 1 question that tests whether a chatbot is really what's needed
- 1 question you'd ask by *watching* rather than asking

Template: [`templates/discovery-question-bank.md`](../../templates/discovery-question-bank.md)

### 3. Problem statement — `problem-statement.md`
One page. It must state **who**, **what**, **current cost**, **baseline**, **target** and **constraints**, and it must **not** name a solution. Mark any numbers you've assumed.

Template: [`templates/problem-statement.md`](../../templates/problem-statement.md)

### 4. Success metrics and open questions — `success-metrics.md`
- **Three** measurable success metrics, each with: definition, how it's measured, baseline, target, time frame
- Your **top five unknowns** (things you don't know yet that could change the solution)
- A one-paragraph reply to the director explaining, politely, why you're not starting with a chatbot, and what you propose instead

## Done when

- [ ] Stakeholder map includes at least one stakeholder not named in the brief
- [ ] 15 discovery questions meet the mix above
- [ ] Problem statement names no technology or solution
- [ ] Each success metric has a baseline, a target and a measurement method
- [ ] Your reply to the director is respectful, under 150 words, and proposes a next step

## Self-check rubric

| Criterion | 1 — Needs work | 2 — Good | 3 — Excellent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem focus | Restates the request | Identifies the follow-up problem | Distinguishes symptoms from root causes (duplicates, scattered data, no tracking) |
| Measurability | Vague goals | Metrics with targets | Metrics with baselines, methods and realistic time frames |
| Stakeholder insight | Lists names | Notes interests | Anticipates resistance and missing stakeholders |
| Communication | Technical or blunt | Clear | Clear, empathetic, and moves the client forward |

When you're done, compare with the [reference solution](build-solution.md).
