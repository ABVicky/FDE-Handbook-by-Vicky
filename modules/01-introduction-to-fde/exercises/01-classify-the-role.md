# Exercise 01 — Classify the role

For each scenario, decide which role is speaking: **Software engineer · AI engineer · Solutions engineer · Implementation consultant · Technical PM · FDE**. Write one sentence explaining your choice.

1. "I spent the week inside the hospital's network getting our platform to read their lab results system, then trained the night-shift nurses on the new dashboard."
2. "I prepared a 30-minute demo using sample data and answered the CIO's security questions so we could close the contract."
3. "I configured the CRM's workflows and permission sets according to the client's requirements document."
4. "I rewrote the ranking model and cut median latency from 900 ms to 300 ms for all users of our search product."
5. "I interviewed eight customers and wrote the spec for next quarter's reporting feature."
6. "The bank's compliance team rejected our first design, so I rebuilt the pipeline to run entirely inside their cloud account. Their analysts now use it daily, and review time is down 30%."
7. "I refactored the billing service and added integration tests."
8. "After the pilot, I documented everything and handed it to the customer's internal team, then wrote up the three reusable connectors for our product team."

**Bonus:** two of these could reasonably be argued as more than one role. Which ones, and why?
