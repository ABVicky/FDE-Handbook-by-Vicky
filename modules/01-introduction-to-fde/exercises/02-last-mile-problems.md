# Exercise 02 — Find the last-mile problems

For each organization below, a vendor has just sold them a powerful tool. List **at least four** things that would stop the tool from delivering value without engineering work inside the organization. Then pick the one you'd tackle first and say why.

### A. A 40-branch pharmacy chain buys an AI demand-forecasting tool
Context: each branch orders stock by phone; sales data lives in three different point-of-sale systems bought over 15 years.

### B. A city hospital buys an AI assistant to answer staff questions about hospital policies
Context: policies are 2,000 PDFs and Word files on a shared drive; some are outdated; some are restricted to senior staff.

### C. A logistics company buys a customer-support chatbot
Context: customers mostly ask "where is my parcel?"; tracking data is in a legacy system with no API; agents currently look it up in a desktop app.

### D. Your own choice
Pick an organization you know personally: a school, a shop, your workplace. Invent a plausible tool purchase and do the same analysis.

**Format your answer as a table:**

| Problem | Why the tool alone can't solve it | What engineering work is needed |
