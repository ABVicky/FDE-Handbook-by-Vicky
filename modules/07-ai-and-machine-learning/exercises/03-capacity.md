# Exercise 03 — From scores to a decision under capacity

Counsellors can make about 60 first calls a day; on peak days ~90 leads arrive, so a third wait until tomorrow.

On the 2025 intake, using a model trained on 2024, compare three orders for day-one calls: random, by score, first come first served. For each: how many future enrolled students are reached on day one?

Then write the paragraph you'd send Mr. Fernandes: what ordering to use, how much it helps, and what would help more.
