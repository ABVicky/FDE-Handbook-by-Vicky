# Module 09 — Field Track: The security questionnaire

**Time:** ~2 hours · **Capabilities:** C4, C5

## Why

Before go-live, most organizations send a **security questionnaire**: dozens of questions about data, access, encryption, vendors and incidents. Answering it well (specific, honest, quick) is often what decides whether the project launches this month or next quarter. FDEs answer these constantly.

## The task

Mr. Okafor has sent the questionnaire below. Answer every question for AdmitFlow as built through this module, in `my-work/field-track/m09-questionnaire.md`. One to four sentences each. Where the honest answer is "not yet," say so and give the plan and date.

1. What personal data does the system store, and where (provider, region)?
2. Is data encrypted in transit? At rest? How?
3. How do users authenticate? Is MFA enforced?
4. How are leavers' accounts disabled, and how quickly?
5. What roles exist, and what can each do?
6. Which third parties receive our data? What data, and under what terms?
7. Is any of our data used to train AI models?
8. Can the AI features send messages or change records without a person approving?
9. How are API keys and passwords stored and rotated?
10. What is logged? Do logs contain personal data?
11. How are backups made, where are they kept, and when was a restore last tested?
12. How would you detect and respond to a data breach? Who would contact us, and how fast?
13. Has the system been penetration-tested?
14. Can we export all our data and delete it at the end of the contract?

## Done when

- [ ] Every question answered specifically; no "industry standard security"
- [ ] "Not yet" answers have a plan and a date
- [ ] A peer playing Mr. Okafor asks three follow-up questions, and you answer them in writing
