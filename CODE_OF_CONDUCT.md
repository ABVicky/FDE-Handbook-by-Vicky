# Code of conduct

This handbook is for people at every stage, including complete beginners. Everyone taking part (in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions) is expected to:

- **Be respectful and patient.** Assume good intent. There are no stupid questions here.
- **Be constructive.** Critique work, not people. Explain why, and suggest a way forward.
- **Be inclusive.** Welcome people of every background, identity and level of experience. Use plain language; explain jargon.
- **Respect privacy.** Never post anyone's personal data, including real client or student data in examples.
- **Give credit.** Cite sources; don't copy others' work without permission.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes, sexualized content, deliberate intimidation, or publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately (see the README's author and platform contacts). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, or block participants who break this code.
