# Behavioural questions

Answer with **situation → what you did → result → what you learned**, in under two minutes. Use real examples: your capstone, Field Track interviews, break & fix incidents, work or volunteering.

Common FDE questions:
1. Tell me about a time a client asked for something that wasn't what they needed.
2. Describe a time you had to work with incomplete information or an unfamiliar system.
3. Tell me about something you shipped that didn't work as expected. What did you do?
4. Describe a disagreement with a stakeholder. How was it resolved?
5. When have you said no to a request? How?
6. Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology quickly to deliver something.
7. How do you decide when something is good enough to ship?
8. Describe a time you made a mistake that affected a user or client.

Prepare six stories that between them cover discovery, technical depth, a failure, a conflict, communication and ownership. Most questions can be answered with one of them.
